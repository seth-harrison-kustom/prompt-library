User Guide

Kustom Voice

Say what you need, call out who to tag - the note lands on the job in DASH faster than typing it ever could.
PILOT · YOU GO FIRST

You're on the move all day and you know exactly what's happening on your jobs. Kustom Voice gets it on the record the fast way: you talk, it listens - your words appear on screen as you speak. It turns what you say into a clean, professional DASH note, tags the right people, and posts it to the job. Even with no signal: your note saves and sends itself when you're back in coverage. The app has a quick swipe-through tour when you first open it (and behind the ? anytime) - this guide goes deeper on what everything means.

First time - 1 minute

STEP 1
On your phone, open kustom-voice.vercel.app
STEP 2
Sign in with your Kustom Microsoft account (same as email).
STEP 3
Bookmark it. Take the 30-second tour that pops up - it shows every screen.

Two kinds of notes

● Daily Report
Your update on a job. The app shows a short list of what's helpful to cover for that job type - just speak to those points.
"Hung drywall in the kitchen and living room, painting tomorrow, waiting on the vanity."
● General Note
Any note, any role - a call recap, a decision, an instruction. Say whatever you need and it writes it up clean. No list, no format.
"Talked to the adjuster, they approved the supplement, tell the coordinator to reschedule."

Find your job - three ways

Customer name default
Pick your division (type or say the city or code), then a last name - or tap the mic and say it all at once: "Smith in Atlanta." Each result offers Record Note (straight in), Review to Confirm (see the team + recent notes first), or Open in DASH.
Job number
Tap Job # and type just the digits - it adds the dashes. Or say it out loud. Works on brand-new jobs the database hasn't synced yet, and with no signal at all.

Recording - what you'll see

Tap ● Record and talk the way you'd brief a coworker - rambling is fine, it does the cleanup. Your words appear as you speak:
Live transcript
Hung drywall in the kitchen and living room, painting starts tomorrow
Stop · 00:42
Review →
The mic bar bounces green when it hears you. Red = it's hearing silence.
Say who to loop in as you go: "tag Mike Dean," "make sure the coordinator sees this." When you tap Stop, it takes a beat to double-check your words - Continue lights up the instant it's ready, so you can't cut yourself off. Forgot something? Record more adds to the same note. Changed your mind? Clear & start over asks first - and has Undo. Accidentally refreshed the page? Everything's still there.

Review - reading the tag chips

The review screen shows the clean write-up (edit anything), the DASH header it detected, and who it will tag. The chips tell you exactly how each person matched:
Tag on the DASH note
✓ Jessica Graham · Est ✕ ✓ Mike Dean K ✕ ⚠ Seth ✕
✓ + role = on this job's team (that's their role) · ✓ + K = a Kustom teammate, found in the company directory · ⚠ = tap it - if more than one person matches, you'll pick the right one from a list.
Add more people by typing or with the 🎤 Add by voice button - nicknames are fine ("Mike" finds Michael), and full names match best. Headers show as chips too - drop a wrong one, add a missed one:
POST INSPECTION ✕ + Add header ▾
Then Send to DASH - the note posts to the job with your name on top, usually within a couple minutes.

No signal? Briefcase mode

Dead zone at the site? Everything still works. Enter the job #, record - your note saves to the phone. You'll see this in the header until it sends:
🧳 1 waiting
The moment you're back in coverage it flips to "Sending…" and posts itself - nothing else to do.
While it waits, the saved screen lets you add tags and a DASH header - the same smart name matching works completely offline. It already tags the people you named while talking; this is for confirming or adding specific ones. (Your words get written out when it sends - no signal, no transcript yet.) When a briefcase note posts, you get one email recapping what went in, who got tagged, and anyone it couldn't find.

Tagging & privacy - the rules that never bend

🔒 Always Private

Every note posts Private. There's no way to make one public through this tool.

🔒 Customer is never tagged

The homeowner, insured, adjuster and any outside party are blocked - always, even if asked.

Name or role - both work

Say a person ("Mike Dean") or a role ("the coordinator," "the GM," "PC") - it finds whoever holds it on the job, or anyone at Kustom by full name.

CE only when you ask

Say "tag CE" and Centralized Estimating gets tagged. Official headers add CE automatically - remove the chip if you don't want it, and that's honored.

Nobody dropped silently

Can't match someone you named? The note says so, still posts, and you're emailed so you can forward it.

Your name rides on top

DASH posts from a shared account, so every note opens with "Recorded by [you] via Kustom Voice" - the team always knows it's yours.

💬

Say full names when you can. "Mike Dean," not just "Mike" - it matches better (nicknames are fine: "Mike" finds Michael). One-and-only first names auto-complete: there's only one Arcadia at Kustom, so saying "Arcadia" will auto-select her.

¿Hablas español?

Tap your initials (top right) → Español - the whole app switches, including the tour. Record and review your note in Spanish; when it posts, the office gets the English translation on top with your original Spanish preserved below. Best of both.

Good to know

Job not found?Brand-new jobs sync overnight. Check the digits and tap continue anyway - it still delivers. And if a teammate already sent a note for that job, you'll see a green confirmation that notes are reaching it.
Mic bar red?It's hearing silence - unmute or uncover the mic and re-record. Green means it's got you.
First note feels slow to show words?The first recording after opening the app warms up for a second or two - everything you said is still captured, the screen just catches up.
Accidentally refreshed?Relax - your recording and your edits are still there, on both the record and review screens.
Next note on the same job?The sent screen has "Another note - same job" plus a search box for the next one - no backtracking.
Want the tour again?Tap the ? up top anytime.
